- `flk delegate wait` (and `start`/`send --await`) no longer reports `no_result` (exit 5) while a Claude delegate is still working (#1257). When the screen read idle mid-turn — every Claude pane did for a minute after a TUI client detached — the await settled, found no reply inside its grace and called the round empty. `agent.result` now reports `turn_open_at_ms` (also as `data.turn_open_at_ms` on a `no_result` refusal) while Claude's transcript records the newest turn as still running, and the await settles again instead of ending the round. Claude's `finished` now follows the transcript's own end of a turn: its `turn_duration` entry, or one of the shapes Claude closes a turn with and no such entry (a resumed session's `No response requested.`, an interrupt, a local command, a compaction). Once a session has written a `turn_duration` entry, a narration between two tool calls is no longer read as the turn's reply. Codex delegates are not covered (#1268). Transcript times keep their milliseconds.
