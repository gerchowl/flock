- Every agent now records where its questions go: `ask_human`, `ask_operator`, `route` or `deny` (ADR-0034 §2, #1179). An agent started from an agent's pane defaults to `route`, an operator start to `ask_human`, and a `--unattended` start to `ask_operator`. `flk agent start`, `flk agent fork` and `flk delegate start` take `--questions` and `--unattended`, and the MCP tools `flock_agent_start` and `flock_agent_fork` take `questions`. An agent may only narrow a delegate's policy to `route` or `deny`; anything wider is refused with `questions_widening_refused` and exit 13 before anything is created. The policy shows in `agent get` and `delegate list`, survives a restart, a resume, a hibernation and a live handoff, and reaches the agent's pane as `FLOCK_QUESTIONS` (unset for `ask_human`). Nothing acts on it yet: the question hooks come in later slices of #1160.
