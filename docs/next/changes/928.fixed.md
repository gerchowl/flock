- A mesh hub that took custody of a message and then lost every route except one leading back through a node the message already crossed now reports it. After 30 seconds without a loop-free route, long enough for a flapping edge to come back, the hub ends its custody and signs `undeliverable` with `loop_detected` for the sender, who sees it in `msg.status` and `msg.wait_reply` within seconds instead of `custody` until the custody deadline days later. A route that turns loop-free within that time is taken as before, and a hub with no route at all still waits for one. No protocol bump. (#928)
