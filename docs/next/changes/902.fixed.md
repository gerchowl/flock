- Report answers and receipts that die at a forwarding hub. An answer whose custody ends at a hub without being passed on now gets the same hub-signed `undeliverable` receipt as a forwarded message, so `msg.status` on the answer at the replier reports `undeliverable`, with the reason and the hub in `detail`, instead of `custody` until its own deadline. A delivery or read receipt that dies at a hub is reported back to the node that signed it, which routes it once more on the current route. If that one dies too, the signer gives up: `msg.status` on `receipt:<message id>:<state>` there shows `undeliverable`, and the sender keeps its last known state. A hub never reports an `undeliverable` receipt itself, so these outcomes cannot bounce between hubs. No protocol bump: a v1.0.0 replier or signer refuses the hub's receipt once, and the hub drops it.
