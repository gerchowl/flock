- Injection lock (#850): while flock types into an agent pane and presses Enter (inbox wake, `agent send --submit`, `pane.submit`, `delegate start`), that pane's keystrokes and pastes, from an attached client or from `pane send-*` / `agent send` calls, are held in order and replayed as soon as the injection ends. Esc or Ctrl-C ends the hold at once and abandons the injection. The hold never lasts longer than 7 seconds, and the pane's border reads `flock is typing…` meanwhile. For an inbox wake or `agent send --submit`, a one-row draft in a Claude, Codex or OpenCode composer is cleared, the sentence is submitted, and the draft is typed back and read back exactly. The draft is held in flock's memory, never the clipboard. A draft flock cannot put back is kept and surfaced in a notification. `flk pane stash show|restore <pane>` (socket `pane.stash_show` / `pane.stash_restore`) shows or restores it, and a live handoff carries the stash. Submission evidence gains `draft`. Turn it off with `[ui.injection_lock] enabled = false`.
